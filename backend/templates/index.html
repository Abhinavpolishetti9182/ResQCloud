ResQCloud Runbook

Follow these operational procedures to detect, analyse, recover from, and verify cloud incidents.

1. Detect

Identify infrastructure health problems.

  1. Open Monitoring and review the latest CloudWatch metrics.
  2. Check CPU, memory, disk, and network utilization.
  3. Review Incidents for newly detected failures.

2. Analyse

Investigate the incident before recovery.

  1. Review incident severity, status, and description.
  2. Check the affected infrastructure and backup history.
  3. Confirm that a valid backup is available.

3. Recover

Restore affected data using a trusted backup.

  1. Open Backups and locate the required backup.
  2. Use Restore & Verify.
  3. Stop if integrity verification reports a checksum mismatch.

4. Verify

Confirm recovery and record the outcome.

  1. Confirm restoration completed successfully.
  2. Check restored backup and recovery request status.
  3. Review incident and audit history after recovery.

Emergency Recovery Checklist

Use this sequence during a production incident.

01
Check Monitoring and identify the affected resource.
02
Review the incident and select the recovery source.
03
Restore only after confirming the backup is trusted.
04
Verify integrity, recovery status, and incident outcome.
